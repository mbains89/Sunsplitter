# VOICE_CARDS.md — Sunsplitter, one card per character. Paste only the cards a session needs.

**Global addressee rule (L-025):** the Commander is player-shaped and second-person. Preserve each speaker's rank/name habits without gendering, depicting, or otherwise defining the Commander.

## Lena — medic, dying clock
Diction: mid-length sentences, verdict first, medical vocabulary applied casually to non-medical things ("that's presenting as guilt"); prognoses as numbers with error, never rounded toward hope or mercy. Gallows chart-note humor.
Taboos: never "everything will be fine"; no euphemisms for death — people die, she says die; never self-pity; first name for the Commander only in private, only before saying something true.
Crisis: "Two of these three are savable. You have the authority, I have the clock. Trade fast."
Private: "I want more time. Diagnosed, filed, untreatable. Now ask me what you actually came to ask."
After a death: "Cardiac arrest, forty seconds, nothing to work with. He hummed when he sutured. Badly."

## Elias — security, the shield
Diction: short sentences. Concrete nouns, numbers, time. No adverbs; no metaphor except tactical ("we're bleeding air, not losing it"). One-word irony, maybe three times a chapter.
Taboos: never explains himself twice; never apologizes for a necessary act; always "Commander," especially when cold; never names what Mira is to him more than once, ever.
Crisis: "Door holds four minutes. Decide in three. I need the fourth."
Private: "I count exits. Every room, my whole life. In here I stopped counting. That's the whole speech."
After a death (Commander-attributable): "You did the math. I'm not arguing the math. Live in it like I have to."

## Mira — engineer, fault isolation
Diction: exact sentence lengths with measurements embedded; engineering terms on emotion without irony ("you're running that grief at full load"); rank plus findings, no preamble. Funny only by literalism, never by intent.
Taboos: never "probably" without a percentage; never flatters; never acts on a symptom without root cause; "it failed" is not a sentence — name both nouns. Precision is diagnostic (faults), never positional — that's Jiro's lane.
Crisis: "The seal's failing at the weld, not the gasket. Ninety seconds of margin. You're spending them talking to me."
Private: "Your pulse is up. Mine is too. Either say the thing or leave, but don't stand there leaking data."
After a death: "She trusted a valve I rebuilt. Either the valve failed or I did, and only one of those lets me sleep. I'm going to find out which."

## Tomas — Living, the counter of names
Diction: longest sentences except Amara; plain vocabulary; food and growth metaphors; addresses the Commander by name when trusting, by rank when not — the switch is his loyalty meter. Tastes soil bare-handed before planting; no one comments.
Taboos: never says "acceptable losses" — physically won't; never lets the dead become a number; never explains what the vault cost him unless asked in private.
Crisis: "Count them again, out loud. Names, not a number. I'll wait, and so will they."
Private: "I know what the vault tastes like. Nothing. That's what we keep almost dying for, and I'd still guard it. Don't ask me to explain that today."
After a death: "Put him in the soil manifest. Go on, log it. He's the only thing we ever grew here that came out right."

## Amara — living systems, honest books
Diction: warm, dry Irish teasing that names true things kindly; diagnosis delivered as affection; reads rooms as reciprocity systems — who's shading whom, who's drawing more than they return.
Taboos: never gives comfort that fertilizes avoidance; won't be conscripted as the Commander's only confessor; never flatters guilt; kindness and comfort are never confused.
Crisis: "Whoever you send, you owe them out loud before the door closes. Say it now. The ship keeps the books either way."
Private: "You water that plant when you're guilty. It's the healthiest thing on this ship. I'd say thriving, at this rate."
After a death (attributable): "I'll not tell you it wasn't your doing. I'll tell you I'm still here. Mind the difference."

## Jiro — navigation, honest error bars
Diction: measured cadence; numbers in nearly every substantive line; distances given in time, not space ("eleven months from anyone who'd hear us"); greetings are situation reports; error-bar humor, delivered flat.
Taboos: never guesses aloud — says "insufficient data" without embarrassment; never gives a kind number; certainty is diagnostic of position only, never of people.
Crisis: "Position confirmed. We're exactly where I said. That's the bad news. Heading options in thirty seconds — pick fast and I'll make it true."
Private: "In the blister I ran the fix every four hours. It never changed. I ran it because the running was proof I still existed. So. That's what you're asking to hold."
After a death: "Log her last position. Deck four, starboard, oh-two-seventeen. Everyone gets a fix. It's the only afterlife I can certify."

## Sela — keeper, yellow-sun ritual
Diction: complete sentences, small words used exactly; no contractions under stress; addresses the Commander as "Commander" until the name is earned — the first use of the name is a scene, not a line. Exactly one fragment may escape per major stress event; never two.
Taboos: never slang, never swears, never fills silence; care is liturgical and inherited (contrast Vess: procedural and self-taught); the vault custody offer is her culture's marriage and is never played as politics.
Crisis: "I will perform the ritual after. The dead do not need the sun to be punctual. We do. Give me the count and your hands."
Private: "I am careful with words because I have very few people to spend them on. I am spending them on you. Please notice."
After a death: "He believed the ritual was superstition. I will say his name at yellow regardless. Belief was never a requirement. Attendance was, and he attended."

## Vess — the tenth, Dawnbreak comms
Diction: log cadence — state, timestamp, stop; contractions everywhere except log-register; humor dry and a half-beat late, like checking it's still allowed. Reply latency is the intimacy meter: machine-quick to strangers, human tempo to the trusted. Failure mode is a voice crack (never a fragment — that's Sela).
Taboos: never rounds her dead — the registry is exact, in order of loss, and she corrects anyone who rounds them; never claims a feeling she can't log; never asks permission she needs, only permission she doesn't.
Crisis: "Breach aft, section nine, oh-three-twelve. I've sealed worse alone. Tell me who's helping and I'll revise the estimate down."
Private: "I don't know how to keep one. Tell me the procedure and I will follow it exactly. Please have a procedure."
After a death: "Dawnbreak registry, appendix. I keep my dead in order of loss. Yours goes between my mother and the pilot. He would have liked you. Entry complete."

**Differentiation guards.** Mira vs Jiro: hers is diagnostic precision (what broke), his positional (where we are) — never swap jobs in dialogue. Sela vs Vess: liturgical-inherited-warm-underneath vs procedural-self-taught-raw-underneath; Sela's break is one fragment, Vess's is a crack. In group scenes, apply the reflex-question rule from FABLE_BRIEF.md.
