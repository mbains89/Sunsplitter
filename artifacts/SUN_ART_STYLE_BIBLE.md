# Sunsplitter — Art style bible (one page)

Anchors: `images/<id>.jpg` + `images/bodysuit_<id>.jpg` (living cast).
Vess face = `images/vess.jpg` only (L-029). Mira = L-030 ice-blue, no Amara
freckles. Commander faceless (L-025).

## Medium

Photoreal cinematic still, not illustration, not anime, not catalog 3D beauty
render. Looks like a high-end game/cinematic photograph: real skin pores,
hair strand variation, matte-to-satin tactical fabric, recessed glow seams.
Portrait aspect **~784×1168 (2:3)**. Event plates are unique beats, not cropped
portraits.

## Lighting

Low-key cinematic. Soft cool key on the face (front-left / slightly above).
Magenta–purple rim on hair and far shoulder. Suit glow is a secondary fill on
jaw/collar only — do not blow the face. Atmospheric haze or dust in volume,
not studio seamless (exception: body_ref pack uses a plain studio backdrop).
Avoid harsh pure-red key; restrained emergency red may exist in the bay.

## Palette

Base: deep black, charcoal, gunmetal. Skin and hair stay natural, not graded
into fashion teal. Environment accents: cyan/teal strips + distant purple +
sparing warm emergency points. **Suit neon is per-character**, copied from
that person’s CURRENT bodysuit (Lena/Mira cyan; Vess violet; Tomas/Amara
amber/orange, etc.). Do not recolor every suit to one house neon.

Living-warm vs Future-cold poles stay in the environment, not in the face.

## Lens

~85mm-class portrait; eye-level or slight below. Shallow depth of field:
eyes and near suit/skin sharp; background bokeh of rectangular bays, conduit,
and vertical light strips. No Dutch-angle hero poster. No ultra-wide face
distortion.

## Sharpness / grain

Pin-sharp iris, lashes, freckles/pores, fabric weave. Fine cinematic grain in
shadows — not plastic smoothness, not heavy digital noise, not beauty-app
skin. Hair edges stay fibrous. Thumbnail test: identity must survive.

## Ban list

Curved-ring O’Neill interiors; baked names / ship names / “BATCH A” / system
copy / ending titles; Commander face or gendered Commander body; generic
crew extras; beautify / age-shift / ethnicity swap; green-eyed or freckled
Mira; porcelain/discarded Vess; official portrait pose as an event stand-in;
neutral arms-at-sides catalog stare in event scenes; studio seamless on
event plates; softening adult beats; invented body_ref paths; generating
event or body_ref binaries in Cursor or Grok Bot.
