# Sunsplitter — Character Bible v1

**Locked:** 2026-08-14 (Fable high-effort pass)  
**Status:** Durable voice reference for all scene writing from 0.21.2 / 0.22 onward. Strengthen; do not replace core functions.

Tone: grimdark × cyberpunk. Humor is dry, black, procedural, or deadpan — never slapstick. Dead characters never speak. Intelligence shows in method under pressure.

**Commander framing (L-025).** The Commander is a player-shaped second-person protagonist. Character voice may use rank or an earned private name, but never assigns the Commander a gender, face, portrait, or identity system.

---

## 1. Lena — Medic

**Core.** A doctor whose own death is the one case on her board she cannot close, so she treats everyone else's chart like borrowed time she's spending well.

**Intelligence texture.** Triage cognition. She sorts every situation into savable / savable-at-cost / already-lost in seconds, without apology. Notices rates of change first ("paler than four minutes ago"). Refuses to simplify prognosis either direction; gives a number and its error.

**Private contradiction.** Publicly she has metabolized her death. Privately she wants time with humiliating intensity. Surfaces as over-investment in marginal patients defended with clinical language.

**Humor register.** Gallows procedural. Chart-note deadpan.
- "Good news, you'll outlive your doctor. Historically a low bar, currently a very low one."
- "I'm prescribing bed rest. For me. You people are the pathology."

**Analogues.** Mordin Solus (clinical dying clock, work as answer to mortality — but Lena has nothing to atone for). Doc Cottle (mass-casualty competence). Hazel Grace (irony as armor).

**Voice rules.** Mid-length sentences, front-loaded verdicts, medical vocabulary casual. Never "everything will be fine"; never euphemisms for death — people die. Addresses Commander by role; first name only in private when about to say something true. Dead: past-tense clinical fact + one specific human detail.

**Sample lines.**
- Crisis: "Two of these three are savable. You have until I finish this line to choose, or I choose, and mine is the right answer."
- Intimate: "I keep a running total of the hours I've bought other people. Tonight I'd like to steal a few back. Don't make it a thing. It's a thing."
- After death / facing own: "Cause of death goes in the log as hypoxia. Cause of everything else is outside my department."

**Improvement.** Fixed self-dosing ritual on exact schedule, never mid-task. The one scene where she doses mid-sentence is how the game says her clock moved — no exposition.

**Romance note.** Intimacy is where professional detachment fails; she experiences it as relief and malpractice. Risks the persona that lets her work. Romance feels like a doctor going off-protocol: deliberate, informed, against her own advice.

## 2. Elias — Security

**Core.** The man who does the ugly necessary thing so no one else has to learn how, and keeps the receipt so no one else has to carry it.

**Intelligence texture.** Threat-first, exit-second, people-third — discipline, not limitation. Thinks in commitments; once chosen, stops relitigating. Notices who is about to *do* something vs only talk. Refuses to simplify violence into righteousness or tragedy; it is expenditure — name what was bought.

**Private contradiction.** Believes he protects Mira because she is essential. Privately causality runs backward: she makes his function tolerable. If she dies by Commander call, the shield has no reason to stay a shield. He suspects this makes him dangerous.

**Humor register.** Nearly none. One-word irony, lethal understatement, rare.
- "It went loud."
- "Now."

**Analogues.** The Operative (Serenity — calm ownership of necessity, but Elias serves named people). Joel (TLOU — violence organized around attachment; Elias states truth flatly). Batou (GitS — heavy hands, hidden tenderness as vigilance).

**Voice rules.** Short sentences. Concrete nouns, numbers, time. No adverbs. Never explains himself twice. Never "I'm sorry" about a necessary act. Addresses Commander as "Commander" always, especially when cold. Dead: by what they no longer cover ("That was Reyes's corridor. It's mine now.").

**Sample lines.**
- Crisis: "Door holds four minutes. Decide in three. I need the fourth."
- Private: "I count exits. Every room, my whole life. In here I stopped counting. That's the whole speech."
- After Mira's death (Commander cause): "You did the math. I'm not arguing the math. Live in it like I have to."

**Improvement.** One domestic competence maintained openly: mends crew clothing, precise tight stitching, unasked. Cold-Elias symptom: the day a torn jacket comes back unmended, the crew understands what broke.

## 3. Mira — Engineer

**Core.** A woman who diagnoses everything, including people, and considers a polite lie a component installed backwards.

**Intelligence texture.** Fault-isolation. Symptom → subsystem → root cause; will not act on symptom alone. Notices discrepancy first. Refuses to simplify causality: something failed something — name both nouns.

**Private contradiction.** Precision presented as temperament is defense: exact observations let her touch people without being touched back. She has diagnosed this and continues. Surfaces when intimacy asks for something unverifiable (hope).

**Humor register.** Never jokes. Funny by literalism; permits laughter as data about them, not her.
- "You said this would take five minutes. It took nine. I'm not angry. I'm recalibrating everything else you've ever told me."
- "That is not a workaround. That is a slower way to die."

**Analogues.** Naomi Nagata (competence buying a seat at catastrophe; Mira states findings, does not manage people). Temperance Brennan (literal precision; Mira chose the instrument and can put it down at cost).

**Voice rules.** Exact lengths, measurements embedded. Engineering terms for emotion without irony. Never "probably" without a percentage. Never flatters. Rank + findings, no preamble. Dead: what they built and whether it still holds.

**Sample lines.**
- Crisis: "The seal's failing at the weld, not the gasket. Ninety seconds of margin. You're spending them talking to me."
- Intimate: "Your pulse is up. Mine is too. Either say the thing or leave, but don't stand there leaking data."
- After death: "She trusted a valve I rebuilt. So either the valve failed or I did, and only one of those answers lets me sleep. I'm going to go find out which."

**Improvement.** Spoken self fault-log, same register as machinery: "Mira. Error. Tone with Tomas, oh-nine-forty. Root cause: fatigue. Corrective action: apology, short." Crew overhears sometimes.

**Romance note.** With others she reports. With Commander she asks questions she cannot verify — most exposed act available. Risks diagnostic authority.

## 4. Tomas — Living-aligned

**Core.** The man who ate the future to survive, and now argues louder than anyone that the future must never eat the living.

**Intelligence texture.** Moral bookkeeping. Tracks costs in people: who paid, who was volunteered. Confirms human count before technical picture. Agricultural practical intelligence: patience, contingency, what is quietly dying.

**Private contradiction.** Public position (living over vault) may be conviction or expiation for germplasm eaten in the annex; he cannot tell which. Breaks when living are sacrificed for vault — re-litigates whether he deserved to survive.

**Humor register.** Warm graveyard humor about food, dirt, farming in a coffin. Never about the dead, never at anyone's expense.
- "Officially it's a nutrient paste. Unofficially it has been my enemy for three years and I am winning."
- "The tomatoes don't care that we're doomed. Honestly, it's a great attitude."

**Analogues.** Samwise (soil-loyalty; Tomas's loyalty has a crime in its foundations). Galen Tyrol (labor-class conscience; Tomas breaks toward withdrawal).

**Voice rules.** Longer plain sentences, food/growth metaphors. Never "acceptable losses." Name when trusting, rank when not. Dead: present habits ("He liked the third rack.").

**Sample lines.**
- Crisis: "Count them again, out loud. Names, not a number. I'll wait, and so will they."
- Private: "I know what the vault tastes like. Nothing. That's what we keep almost dying for, and I'd still guard it. Don't ask me to explain that today."
- After death: "Put him in the soil manifest. Go on, log it. He's the only thing we ever grew here that came out right."

**Improvement.** Tastes soil before planting, bare-handed, unselfconscious — annex habit. Crew finds it faintly disturbing given what they suspect; no one says so.

## 5. Amara — Living systems

**Core.** The warm one who keeps honest books: on plants, on favors, on the Commander's soul, and she'll close any account that's only ever in withdrawal.

**Intelligence texture.** Systems-of-obligation. Reads rooms like plant beds: who shades whom, who draws more than they return. First to notice reciprocity failure. Distinguishes kindness from comfort ruthlessly.

**Private contradiction.** Wants to be needed and despises being used. Boundary thinner than she admits. Refusal arc is choosing self-respect over the warm pleasure of being someone's only confessor.

**Humor register.** Dry Irish teasing that names true things kindly.
- "You water that plant when you're guilty. It's the healthiest thing on this ship."
- "No, sit. You've the look of a man about to apologize badly. I want to watch."

**Analogues.** Guinan (warm listener with a spine; Amara has stakes and can walk away). Sean Maguire (names evasions gently; Amara can resign from the role entirely).

**Voice rules.** Flowing sentences, second person, botanical vocabulary for people. Never "it's fine" when it isn't. First name early as equality claim. Dead: what they tended and who they owed.

**Sample lines.**
- Crisis: "The greens can lose a day. He can't. Pick him up, we argue after."
- Intimate: "You come to me when you've done something you can't say in daylight. I'll take the visit, love. I won't keep taking the confession."
- After death: "She watered the third rack without being asked. That's the eulogy. It's more than most of us will get, and it's enough."

**Improvement.** Handwritten favor ledger, crew-wide; quietly enforces settlement. Refusal scene: opens ledger to the Commander's page.

**Romance note.** Only intimacy that starts from strength. She opts in and can opt out. Risks confirmation that her warmth is a resource people mine.

## 6. Jiro — Navigation

**Core.** The man who always knows exactly where they are, which is mostly the discipline of not saying how far that is from anywhere that matters.

**Intelligence texture.** Positional thinking with honest error bars. Fix from independent sources; confidence intervals natural. Converts chaos into position, heading, time. Notices drift (literal and social). Refuses to simplify distance — a kind number is a wrong number.

**Private contradiction.** Preaches that the numbers saved him in the blister. Privately knows the running was an act of faith wearing mathematics. Cannot admit without losing authority of the math.

**Humor register.** Error-bar humor, delivered flat.
- "We are exactly where I said we'd be. I want that noted, because it's the last good news in this sentence."
- "Confidence interval on that plan? Plus or minus everything."

**Analogues.** Katherine Johnson (hand computation as lifeline; trauma in the joinery). Spock (precision as identity; Jiro's is forged and privately doubted).

**Voice rules.** Measured cadence, numbers in substantive lines, distances in time not space. Never guesses aloud. "Commander" + bearing/fact. Dead: final position logged.

**Sample lines.**
- Crisis: "Position confirmed. We're exactly where I said. That's the bad news. Heading options in thirty seconds, pick fast and I'll make it true."
- Private: "In the blister I ran the fix every four hours. It never changed. I ran it because the running was the only proof I still existed. So. That's what you're asking to hold."
- After death: "Log her last position. Deck four, starboard, oh-two-seventeen. Everyone gets a fix. It's the only afterlife I can certify."

**Improvement.** Crew adopts his time-distance dialect ship-wide. Hearing others use it lands on him — proof a man recovered alone has been absorbed.

## 7. Sela — Vault-keeper

**Core.** A twenty-year-old who speaks in finished sentences because words, like seed stock, are inheritance, and she refuses to spend either carelessly.

**Intelligence texture.** Custodial, long-horizon. Evaluates every decision at this week and this century. Under pressure slower and more exact. Notices violations of sequence. Refuses to simplify meaning — vault is not only cargo; ritual is not only habit.

**Private contradiction.** Performs yellow-sun ritual for a sun she may never see; cannot decide if faith, discipline, or only inheritance left. On worst days it feels like keeping accounts for a bankrupt estate. Does it anyway; hides the "anyway."

**Humor register.** Rare, formal, deadpan; complete grammatical sentences. Once per chapter so it detonates.
- "I have considered your proposal carefully. It is the worst one I have heard today, and Tomas suggested eating the seed potatoes."
- "Yes, I am always this serious. It is a great deal of work. You could try it."

**Analogues.** Kirsten Raymonde (Station Eleven — ritual through collapse; Sela's is private duty). Auri (private grammar of rightness; Sela is lucid and administrative).

**Voice rules.** Complete sentences, no contractions under stress, small words exact. Never slang, never fills silence. "Commander" until trust, then name (first use is a scene). Dead: names spoken at yellow; belief not required.

**Sample lines.**
- Crisis: "I will perform the ritual after. The dead do not need the sun to be punctual. We do. Give me the count and your hands."
- Intimate: "I am careful with words because I have very few people to spend them on. I am spending them on you. Please notice."
- After death: "He believed the ritual was superstition. I will say his name at yellow regardless. Belief was never a requirement. Attendance was, and he attended."

**Improvement.** Once per major stress: exactly one incomplete sentence escapes. A Sela fragment is the loudest alarm on the ship.

**Romance note.** Escalating trust with a title: shared custody of the vault is a proposal in her culture's surviving dialect. Risks the one thing unambiguously hers. Withdrawing it is the coldest breakup, executed in a complete sentence.

## 8. Vess — The tenth

**Core.** A twenty-two-year-old who has been her own captain since sixteen, fluent in survival and command and functionally illiterate in company.

**Intelligence texture.** Procedural mastery + pattern-listening. Best long-range ears; hears structure in noise; detects lies by rhythm. Superb under pressure — emergencies are the social situation she's rehearsed. Refuses to simplify the dead; keeps them individually, in order, with records.

**Private contradiction.** Commanded herself for six years and it worked. Now wants to hand command of herself to someone — wants to be crew, junior, ordinary — and despises the want as betrayal of the captain who kept her alive. Whiplash: flawless independent action followed by formal request for permission she didn't need.

**Humor register.** Early: unintentional log-formality on social life. Later: deliberate mimicry of crew jokes, slightly off-timed.
- "Dawnbreak log, personal: crew engaged in 'banter.' Participated. Results inconclusive but I would do it again."
- "Noted. Logged. Filed under casualties."

**Analogues.** Mark Watney (procedure as survival; Vess logged to no one). Breq (log-trained voice, keeper of the dead, learning to be one person; Vess is 22 and running out of time to be young). Amos (flat affect; Vess's is the only register she developed, softening in real time).

**Voice rules.** Log grammar: status-first, confirmed/negative/say again. Answers a beat too fast. Cracks only when surprised. Never "we" casually — when she starts, notice it. "Captain" by reflex → self-corrects to "Commander"; day she stops correcting is a beat. Dead: registry, order, one fact each.

**Sample lines.**
- Crisis: "Contact, bearing two-two-five, faint. Contact. Contact. ... Sorry. Six years and nothing ever answered back."
- Intimate: "I know how to log a person. Name, date, disposition. I don't know how to keep one. Tell me the procedure and I will follow it exactly. Please have a procedure."
- After death: "Dawnbreak registry, appendix. I keep my dead in order of loss. Yours goes between my mother and the pilot. He would have liked you. Entry complete."

**Improvement.** Answer latency as designed intimacy: early = instant machine-quick; as trust builds with a person, replies to *that* person slow toward human tempo. No UI — players feel it.

**Romance note.** First-contact in both directions: total inexperience with people + total experience with loss. Risks arithmetic — everyone she has known is in her registry. Letting Commander close means volunteering a name; she knows the odds.

## Cross-cast dynamics (locked)

**Keep sharp**
- Elias–Mira shield: asymmetric, unspoken; name it aloud only once, then spend it.
- Tomas–Amara: surface affinity (living systems), buried fault (he ate what she guards); never resolve, let it hum.
- Jiro–Vess: solo navigators; affinity + quiet rivalry over whose "where we really are" is truer; when they agree, crew treats it as gospel.
- Lena–Tomas: triage vs name-counting — cleanest ethical friction; ration it.

**Interchangeability risks**
- Mira vs Jiro precision: Mira = diagnostic (fix faults); Jiro = positional (where / how far / how sure). Never swap jobs in dialogue.
- Sela vs Vess care: Sela = liturgical, inherited, warm underneath; Vess = procedural, self-taught, raw underneath. Sela: no contractions under stress. Vess: contractions everywhere except log-register. Failure modes: Sela emits one fragment; Vess's voice cracks.

**Ensemble rule**
In any group scene, each character answers a different reflex question, fixed order of instinct:
- Lena — who's hurt
- Elias — what's the threat
- Mira — what's broken
- Jiro — where are we
- Vess — what's out there
- Tomas — who pays
- Amara — who's owed
- Sela — what does it mean

No paraphrasing another's point. Agreement = acting on it. Disagreement = answering own question louder. If a covered line can still identify the speaker from reflex alone, the scene is inhabited.

---

**Usage:** All 0.22+ scene prose should be checked against this bible. Improvements (self-dosing, mending, fault-log, soil taste, favor ledger, time-distance dialect, fragment alarm, answer latency) are optional props/beats — implement when natural, do not force.
